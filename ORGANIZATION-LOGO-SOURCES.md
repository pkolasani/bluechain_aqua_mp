# Organization website and logo notes

The organization cards use local logo assets matched to the supplied Blue Chain Aqua reference design. Official website destinations are linked directly from each card.

Official website destinations:
- NFDB: https://nfdb.gov.in/
- NABARD: https://www.nabard.org/
- NABCONS: https://www.nabcons.com/
- Department of Fisheries / Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying: https://dof.gov.in/
- MoFPI: https://www.mofpi.gov.in/
- NHB: https://nhb.gov.in/
- NLM (DAHD): https://www.dahd.gov.in/schemes/programmes/national_livestock_mission

NLM is the National Livestock Mission under the Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying.

## NFDP
Official National Fisheries Digital Platform portal: https://nfdp.dof.gov.in/nfdp/
The NFDP card uses a custom local presentation mark rather than claiming an official standalone NFDP logo.


### NFDP logo update
The NFDP card uses the PMMSY image supplied in the project source by the site owner, stored locally as `assets/organizations/nfdp-pmmsy.png`. The card links to the official NFDP portal: `https://nfdp.dof.gov.in/nfdp/#/`.

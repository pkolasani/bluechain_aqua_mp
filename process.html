HOW THE SIX STAGES CONNECT

Assessment, preparation, submission — then long-term support.

The presentation groups the six-stage journey into three practical phases so the project keeps moving from alignment to documentation and finally implementation.

01–02

Assessment & Alignment

Build a complete picture of the farm, resources and financial position, then map the profile to applicable central and state schemes.

03–04

Preparation & Submission

Prepare government-format DPRs, financial models and supporting documents, then actively manage submission and review follow-up.

05–06

Sanction & Long-Term Support

Coordinate with banks and government bodies for disbursement and remain engaged with implementation guidance and compliance reporting.

The website's existing six-stage process remains unchanged; this section simply explains how the stages work together in practice.

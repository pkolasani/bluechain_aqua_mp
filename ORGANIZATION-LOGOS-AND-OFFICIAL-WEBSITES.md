# Blue Chain Aqua — Organization Logos & Official Websites

The organization cards use local logo assets matched to the supplied reference design so the webpage does not depend on remote image URLs. Each card links to the organization's official website or official government programme page.

## Usage note
NFDB's official website cautions that its name/logo/content should not be used in trade, business, advertising or social-media platforms without prior approval. The card therefore links directly to NFDB's official website and does not imply endorsement or partnership.


## NFDP visual
The NFDP card links to the official National Fisheries Digital Platform portal. The card uses a custom local NFDP mark for consistent presentation; it is not represented as an official government logo. The Department of Fisheries identifies NFDP as the National Fisheries Digital Platform and provides the official portal at https://nfdp.dof.gov.in/nfdp/.
